# Original human artwork: Pixel-Boy / Ninja Adventure

All 44 PNGs in `ninja/` are unchanged source bytes from Clear Code's **2022-01-23**
distribution of Pixel-Boy's Ninja Adventure artwork. No image generation, drawing,
recoloring or re-encoding was used to create these files.

- Creator: [Pixel-Boy](https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack)
- First-party art repository: https://github.com/pixel-boy/NinjaAdventure
- Distribution/revision: https://github.com/clear-code-projects/Zelda/tree/5f4c57bfbb162d2f19b287a5c24816d93c17e08d
- Archive: `15 - fixes audio.zip` (2.41 MiB)
- License: **CC0**. Attribution is appreciated, not mandatory.
- Original distribution's explicit CC0 notice and artist credit are preserved
  verbatim in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md), with its URL/hash in the manifest. The
  earlier [SOURCE-LICENSE.md](../wonderland_human/ninja/SOURCE-LICENSE.md) remains intact.
- Each original archive path and SHA-256: [provenance.json](provenance.json).

The dated archive, named artist, first-party repository and explicit license are
credible provenance evidence; they are not a forensic guarantee of the artist's
entire historical production process. The creator's itch.io page could not be
re-read from this environment. No assets from the restricted TopDownFantasy pack
or the Fan-tasy trial are used in the new forest scenes or combat layer.

Usage: round trees, rocks, stumps; flame/nova/sparkle battle animation; falling
leaves. Smoke frames and additional leaf poses are retained source frames available
for editing; unused poses are not presented as active effects. Previously credited
Ninja Adventure floors, details, two trees, shadow, and Lorc's movement icons are
referenced in their original project folders rather than duplicated.
