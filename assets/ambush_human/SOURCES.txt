Neko directional cat frames: original xneko/oneko by Masayuki Koba, Tatsuya Kato and contributors (1990s). Historical distribution: https://github.com/tie/oneko ; original README retained. Public-domain status described by that distribution and https://directory.fsf.org/wiki/Oneko .
Sprite-sheet packaging: adryd, https://github.com/adryd325/oneko.js/tree/5281d057c4ea9bd4f6f997ee96ba30491aed16c0 ; MIT notice retained. Original oneko.gif SHA256: f4c5688eb8ee6f22b08ab8df361222307dc6fe2d5bdcfa12582cea694707e66d.
PNG is a lossless RGBA format conversion for Godot; no artwork redrawn, rescaled, synthesized, or enhanced. Runtime coat tinting only. Historical directional poses establish human provenance; no AI detector was used. Used only for walking representations of the established eight ambush cats and Smoki. Their original portraits and gameplay art are retained.
